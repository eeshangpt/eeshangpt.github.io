Open questions

What I'm working through

What does RAG look like beyond a chatbot?

RAG is mostly used behind a chatbot, but it has many other applications. And no two RAG systems are built the same: the ingestion pipeline changes, and so does the retrieval.

How do you make subjective LLM evals quantitative and understandable?

Evaluations tell us how an ML or deep learning algorithm is performing. LLMs, and products built on them, are less deterministic, so they need evaluations too: formulaic ones, and subjective ones as well. Making those subjective evaluations quantitative and understandable for the end user is the problem I’m interested in.
